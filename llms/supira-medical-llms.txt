# Supira Medical

> Supira Medical, Inc. is a clinical-stage medical device company in Los Gatos, California,
> and a portfolio company of the Shifamed medtech innovation hub. It develops a
> next-generation percutaneous ventricular assist device (pVAD) — a catheter-based blood
> pump delivered through a 10 French (~3.3 mm) sheath — for temporary cardiovascular
> hemodynamic support in high-risk percutaneous coronary intervention (HRPCI) and
> cardiogenic shock. The system holds FDA Breakthrough Device Designation and is in U.S.
> clinical study.

## Caveat on the company website

`https://supiramedical.com/` is served behind a SiteGround bot-challenge interstitial: every
path answers HTTP 202 with a short HTML meta-refresh shell and an `sg-captcha: challenge`
header. The site is live and readable in a browser; automated clients, including this one,
are turned away. Nothing in this profile was derived from behind that challenge.
